1. Our Refund Commitment

At BulkReach, we're committed to your satisfaction. We stand behind our platform with a clear and fair refund policy. If our platform isn't the right fit, we'll make the refund process hassle-free.

2. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on your first payment for any paid plan. If you're not completely satisfied within the first 7 days, contact us for a full refund — no questions asked.

Conditions:

  • Request must be made within 7 calendar days of the initial payment
  • Applicable only on the first subscription payment
  • Not applicable if more than 5,000 messages have been sent
  • One refund per user/business account

3. Eligible Refund Scenarios

  • Service Downtime: If platform experiences downtime exceeding 99% SLA (8+ hours/month), you're entitled to a prorated refund.
  • Billing Errors: Incorrect charges, duplicate payments, or wrong plan pricing — full refund of the erroneous amount.
  • Feature Non-Delivery: If a feature explicitly promised at time of purchase is not delivered within 30 days.
  • Technical Issues: Critical platform bugs preventing core functionality, unresolved within 72 hours.

4. Non-Refundable Items

  • Subscription renewals (after the initial 7-day window)
  • Messages already delivered (consumed message packs)
  • Setup fees or onboarding services if already rendered
  • Custom development work already completed
  • Accounts suspended for Terms of Service violations

5. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email billing@bulkreach.in with subject: "Refund Request – [Your Account Email]"
  2. Include your registered email, invoice number, and reason for the refund
  3. Our billing team reviews and responds within 24 business hours
  4. Upon approval, refund is processed via Razorpay within 5 business days

7. Plan Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account dashboard. When you cancel:

  • You retain access to all features until the end of the current billing period
  • No further charges are made after cancellation
  • Your data is retained for 90 days, then permanently deleted
  • You can reactivate within 90 days without data loss

8. Razorpay Refund Timelines

  • UPI / Net Banking: 2–3 business days
  • Credit / Debit Card: 5–7 business days
  • EMI: 7–10 business days
  • Wallets (Paytm, etc.): 1–2 business days
