Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2025

Summary: We collect only what we need to provide our services. We never sell your data. You can request deletion at any time.

1. Information We Collect

When you use BulkReach's services, we collect the following types of information:

1.1 Personal Information

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and business details when you register.
  • Payment Information: Billing details processed securely via Razorpay. We never store your full card details.
  • Communication Data: Messages, support tickets, and feedback you send to us.

1.2 Usage Information

  • Log data including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent
  • Campaign performance data (delivery rates, open rates, click rates)
  • Device information and operating system
  • Referral source and UTM parameters

1.3 Contact Data You Upload

When you upload contact lists for campaigns, this data is stored securely and used solely to deliver your messages. We do not use your contact data for any other purpose.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our WhatsApp marketing platform
  • To process payments securely through Razorpay
  • To send service-related communications (account updates, security alerts)
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve our product
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • To comply with legal obligations under Indian law (IT Act 2000, GDPR if applicable)

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Razorpay: For secure payment processing.
  • Meta Platforms: As a WhatsApp Business API provider, Meta processes message delivery data.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: AWS and Google Cloud for hosting, with strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by Indian courts or regulatory authorities.

4. Data Security

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Regular penetration testing and security audits
  • Multi-factor authentication for all employee access
  • ISO 27001 compliant data handling procedures

5. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Duration of subscription plus 90 days after cancellation
  • Campaign Data: 12 months; can be deleted on request
  • Billing Records: 7 years as required by Indian GST law
  • Log Data: Automatically deleted after 180 days

6. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to processing for marketing purposes

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@bulkreach.in. We respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, analyze website traffic, and measure marketing campaign effectiveness. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Children's Privacy

BulkReach is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We'll notify you of significant changes via email and a prominent notice on our website.
